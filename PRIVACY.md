# Privacy Policy

**Your data never leaves your browser.** Leaguewise does not collect, store, or transmit your
data. It reads your existing ESPN session cookies solely to request your own league's data from
ESPN's fantasy API, exactly as the ESPN website itself does. It also loads player photos from
ESPN's image CDN; those requests contain a public athlete id and no information about you, your
league, or your account.

Leaguewise operates no servers and includes no analytics, telemetry, or tracking of any kind.

Questions: admin@leaguewise.net
